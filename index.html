Help

Pencil — freehand draw.

Eraser — click an object to delete.

Ruler — drag the ruler body to move, drag its end‑handle to rotate, drag along its edge to draw a line.

Compass — drag the top handle to move, drag the pencil tip to set radius, drag the pencil leg around to draw an arc/circle.

Protractor — drag to move/rotate, click two rays to measure an angle.

Select — click an object to select it and see its measurements.

Move — drag a selected object to reposition it.
